Pikes Peak Hill Climb

Car setup
Road © OpenStreetMap contributors · Terrain: USGS 3D Elevation Program
Drag anywhere on the left half to steer. Right side: brake (left pedal) and throttle (right pedal) — press higher for more. Hold the brake at a standstill for reverse. To drift, open Car setup, pick Aids: ABS + traction control and turn traction control down; more torque and a more rearward drive split make it easier. − / + change how much cockpit you see. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Z/X shift, R reset.
